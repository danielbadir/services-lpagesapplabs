01 Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the services.lpagesapplabs.com website and to the client engagement process for LPagesAppLabs custom development services.

02 Marketing Website

The services.lpagesapplabs.com website:

  • Sets no cookies
  • Runs no analytics or tracking scripts
  • Stores no user data on our servers

Fonts are loaded from Bunny Fonts (fonts.bunny.net), a privacy-respecting font CDN operated in the EU. Bunny Fonts sets no cookies and collects no personal data. It is not affiliated with Google.

03 Contact & Enquiries

The contact form on this website generates a mailto: link that opens your local email client. No data is submitted to a server. Your email and message are handled entirely by your own email application.

When you send us an email, we will receive your name, email address, and the content of your message. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and to deliver the requested services.

04 Client Project Data

  • Used exclusively to complete the agreed scope of work

We do not use client materials for any purpose other than delivering the contracted services.

Deliverables may incorporate third-party tools or services (e.g. hosting providers, CDNs, analytics tools) requested or approved by the client. The client is responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of any such tools and ensuring they meet their own compliance requirements.

06 Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email contact@lpagesapplabs.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Email correspondence is retained for up to 3 years for business continuity purposes, then deleted. Project materials are deleted after project handover unless otherwise agreed in writing.
